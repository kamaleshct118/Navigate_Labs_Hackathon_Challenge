### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This policy establishes mandatory anti-bribery standards, ethical conduct rules, intellectual property protection guidelines, and statutory escalation protocols across all enterprise operations worldwide (`Global`). The scope applies universally to all officers, board members, full-time and part-time employees, contractors, consultants, and third-party representatives acting on behalf of the company. Adherence to this policy is strictly enforced to ensure full compliance with the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), UK Bribery Act, EU GDPR, and applicable international anti-corruption regulations.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
Employees must maintain absolute integrity in all corporate dealings and avoid real or perceived conflicts of interest. Gifts over $100 from third parties are strictly prohibited. Acceptance of cash, gift cards, luxury hospitality, or travel expenses from vendors or government officials is entirely forbidden regardless of value. Any business gift or entertainment valued between $50 and $100 received from external commercial entities must be declared in the Corporate Ethics Portal within 5 business days. Furthermore, to safeguard enterprise trade secrets and proprietary data: uploading proprietary corporate source code, internal financial figures, strategic plans, or customer PII to unapproved public AI models or external LLMs is strictly prohibited. Mandatory rule: Regulatory inquiries, subpoenas, or audit notices MUST NOT be answered directly by employees and MUST be immediately escalated to legal-escalations@enterprise.com.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
Declarations and legal incident workflows are processed through the Legal & Compliance Portal following a strict response matrix:
- **Gift Declaration ($50 – $100)**: Submission via Portal -> Ethics Officer review and decision within 3 business days.
- **External Legal / Subpoena Escalation**: Receipt of regulatory inquiry/subpoena -> Immediate email forwarding to `legal-escalations@enterprise.com` -> Legal Operations triage within 2 hours -> General Counsel review and formal response within statutory deadlines.

No employee is authorized to issue verbal or written statements to regulatory bodies or law enforcement agents without direct Legal Counsel representation.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Human Escalations
Violations of anti-bribery laws or unauthorized disclosure of confidential data to public AI platforms will result in immediate termination of employment, forfeiture of equity incentives, and potential referral to civil or criminal law enforcement authorities. Whistleblower protection is strictly guaranteed for any employee reporting ethical concerns in good faith. Any active regulatory investigation, formal court subpoena, search warrant, or high-risk compliance breach MUST be escalated immediately to the Chief Legal Officer (CLO) and the Board Audit & Ethics Committee.
