### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This document defines the legacy framework governing flexible work schedules and technology equipment provisioning for the US New York Headquarters (`US-NY-HQ`). The scope of this policy applies exclusively to full-time regular employees who have successfully completed their mandatory 90-day introductory probation period. Part-time employees, temporary contractors, seasonal staff, and intern personnel are strictly excluded from remote work eligibility under this historical agreement. Employees currently undergoing formal performance improvement plans (PIPs) or residing outside the tri-state commuting area are likewise ineligible for remote working privileges. This document represents the original operating baseline established during the initial post-pandemic return-to-office transition and has since been rendered obsolete by newer enterprise operational standards.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
Eligible full-time personnel are permitted to work remotely for a maximum limit of 2 days per week (specifically Mondays and Fridays, unless an alternate schedule is explicitly agreed upon in writing). The remaining 3 business days must be worked on-site at the NY HQ facility. To support home workspace setup, the company provides a one-time equipment reimbursement of up to $300 for home office setup. Covered items under this historical stipend are strictly limited to external computer monitors, standard office keyboards, computer mice, and ergonomic desk chairs. To claim reimbursement, employees are required to submit original physical receipts within 30 calendar days of item purchase. Submissions made after 30 days will be automatically rejected by Accounts Payable without right of appeal. Recurring home internet, mobile phone charges, peripheral supplies, and utility expenses are explicitly non-reimbursable under this policy version.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
Employees seeking remote work schedules must submit a formal request through the legacy HR Operations Portal. The operational workflow requires direct manager sign-off prior to commencing any remote work arrangements.
1. **Tier 1 (Direct Manager)**: Initial evaluation of job role suitability, schedule alignment, and approval of the requested 2-day remote allocation.
2. **Tier 2 (HR Operations)**: Final verification of employee tenure, probation completion status, and processing of home office equipment reimbursement claims up to the $300 maximum cap.

All physical receipts must be attached to the digital expense submission form and validated against corporate expense audit guidelines before funds are disbursed.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Escalations
Unauthorized remote work exceeding the 2-day weekly limit or working from undisclosed remote locations constitutes a breach of attendance standards and will result in a formal written warning from Human Resources. Attempting to claim non-qualifying personal items or submitting fraudulent expense claims will lead to immediate revocation of remote privileges and referral to Internal Audit. Any exceptions to the standard 2-day remote limit or disputes regarding unapproved equipment reimbursements cannot be settled at the manager level and must be formally escalated to the HR Policy Lead for final administrative determination.
