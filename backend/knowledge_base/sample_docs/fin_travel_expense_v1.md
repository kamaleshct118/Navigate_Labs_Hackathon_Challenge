### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This policy sets forth the official standards, allowable expenditure limits, and reimbursement procedures for enterprise employees undertaking domestic or international business travel. The scope applies globally (`Global`) to all full-time regular employees, executive officers, and authorized business representatives. Temporary workers and external contractors must follow expense guidelines outlined in their specific commercial agreements and are not covered under this standard employee policy.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
All business travel must be justified by clear commercial objectives and booked through the enterprise designated Online Booking Tool (OBT). Air travel standard is Economy Class for short-haul travel; flights over 6 hours qualify for Premium Economy. Business Class travel is strictly reserved for C-Suite executives or international flights exceeding 12 continuous travel hours with prior approval. Hotel accommodations are capped at $250/night in standard markets and $350/night in high-cost tier-1 cities (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo). Daily meal per diem is $75/day in US and €70/day in EU. Receipts are mandatory for all individual expense items over $25. All expense reports must be finalized and submitted within 14 calendar days of travel completion.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
Travel pre-approval and post-travel expense claims must follow a structured financial workflow based on expense magnitude:
- **Standard Travel Expenses ($0 – $500)**: Direct Line Manager review and approval in Expense Portal.
- **Mid-Tier Travel Expenses ($501 – $1,000)**: Department Director sign-off required prior to travel booking.
- **High-Value Travel Expenses (GreaterThan $1,000)**: Individual travel expenses exceeding $1,000 require CFO pre-approval.

Expense claims submitted without mandatory pre-approval or beyond the 14-day window will be flagged for secondary Finance audit.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Human Escalations
Expensing alcohol outside approved business entertainment dinners, personal leisure side-trips, adult entertainment, or luxury upgrades is strictly prohibited and constitutes expense fraud. Late submissions beyond 45 days from travel completion will be permanently forfeited and denied reimbursement. Any requests for travel policy exceptions, emergency travel advances, or expense disputes exceeding standard limits MUST NOT be handled by local admin staff and MUST be escalated directly to the Travel Compliance Lead and Finance Director for formal review.
