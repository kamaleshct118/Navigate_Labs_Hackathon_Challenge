### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This policy establishes standard operating procedures and expenditure authorization thresholds for procuring goods, commercial services, software licenses, IT hardware, and external consulting agreements across all enterprise operating entities globally (`Global`). The scope governs all budget holders, department leads, procurement officers, and employees authorized to initiate financial commitments on behalf of the company. It applies to all expenditure categories regardless of funding source or geographic branch location.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
To ensure commercial transparency, financial discipline, and fiscal accountability, procurement activities must strictly adhere to authorization limits based on total contract value (TCV) including multi-year commitments:
- **Low-Value Purchases**: Hardware purchases under $2,500 require Manager approval. Purchases must be placed through approved corporate catalogs using corporate purchasing cards (P-Cards).
- **Mid-Tier Software & Equipment**: Software licenses under $5,000 require IT Director approval to prevent shadow IT proliferation and ensure software license compliance.
- **High-Value Contracts & RFPs**: Any software or vendor contract exceeding $10,000 requires a formal RFP with 3 competitive bids, Legal review, and CFO sign-off.

Splicing larger purchase orders into smaller sub-threshold orders ("order splitting") to bypass approval controls is strictly forbidden and monitored via automated ERP audit rules.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
Requisitions must be routed through the Procurement ERP module following the formal threshold authorization workflow:
- **Level 1 ($0 – $2,500)**: Direct Line Manager sign-off via ERP requisition queue.
- **Level 2 ($2,501 – $10,000)**: IT Director / Department Director sign-off + Finance Controller validation.
- **Level 3 (GreaterThan $10,000)**: Procurement Evaluation Committee review (minimum 3 competitive RFP bids) -> Corporate Legal Counsel contract review -> CFO final execution sign-off.

Vendor onboarding requires submission of tax documentation (W-9 / W-8BEN), banking verification, and compliance screening prior to issuing purchase orders.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Human Escalations
Executing vendor contracts without completing the required approval matrix or bypassing formal RFP rules for purchases exceeding $10,000 invalidates the purchase order and renders the contract unenforceable against corporate funds. Single-source vendor selection exceptions (where 3 competitive bids cannot be obtained due to proprietary technical lock-in) require written waiver approval from the Chief Procurement Officer. Any disputes, allegations of vendor conflict of interest, or contract values exceeding $100,000 MUST be escalated directly to the CFO and Corporate Ethics Board.
