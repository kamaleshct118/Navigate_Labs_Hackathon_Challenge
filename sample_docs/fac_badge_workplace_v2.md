### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines physical security standards, building access privileges, and visitor management protocols across all enterprise-owned and leased office facilities globally (`Global`). The scope applies to all employees, tenant contractors, maintenance staff, and external visitors entering corporate premises. Standard facility operating hours are established from 06:00 to 22:00 local time, Monday through Friday. Access outside standard hours or during weekends requires special security authorization.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
To safeguard corporate assets and personnel, all individuals must display visible photo identification badges at all times while on company grounds. Tailgating (entering behind another badged individual without scanning) is strictly prohibited. External visitors must be registered 24 hours prior via Facilities Portal. Unregistered drop-in visitors will be denied entry at security turnstiles. All visitors must sign in at reception, present valid government-issued photo ID, and remain escorted by a badged host employee at all times. Physical access badges are personal and strictly non-transferable. Replacement for lost access badges costs $25 and requires immediate notification to Facilities Security to deactivate lost credential chips and prevent facility compromise.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
Physical access provisioning and visitor approvals follow a three-tier operational workflow managed by Site Security:
- **Standard Facility Access (06:00 – 22:00)**: Automatically granted to full-time employees upon onboarding completion.
- **Visitor Pre-Registration**: Host Employee logs visitor details in Portal -> Security desk generates temporary guest QR badge -> Reception verifies ID upon arrival.
- **After-Hours Facility Access (22:00 – 06:00 & Weekends)**: Requires written requisition submitted 48 hours in advance, approved by Department Manager and Site Facilities Lead.

Access to restricted zones (e.g., server rooms, executive suites) requires secondary approval from the Global Infrastructure Manager.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Human Escalations
Loaning an access badge to an unauthorized colleague or external visitor is a critical security breach resulting in immediate badge confiscation and mandatory HR disciplinary action. Unescorted visitors found in restricted operational areas will be escorted off the property immediately. Any physical security breaches, unauthorized forced entries, or property theft incidents MUST be escalated immediately to the Chief Facilities Officer and Global Physical Security SME.
