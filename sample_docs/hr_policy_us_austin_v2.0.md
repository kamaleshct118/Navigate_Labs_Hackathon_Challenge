### Section 1: Purpose & Eligibility Scope
This document serves as a localized branch addendum to the overarching United States Human Resources Master Policy. It applies specifically to all full-time and part-time regular employees whose primary tax location and office assignment is the Austin, Texas facility (`US-TX-Austin`). Contract personnel, interns, and temporary third-party staff are governed by separate vendor agreements and are excluded from these local benefits. New hires become eligible for standard paid time off accruals immediately upon their start date, though leave usage during the initial 90-day introductory probation period remains subject to local department manager approval.

### Section 2: Policy Rules & Financial Thresholds
In accordance with local market competitive standards and regional workplace regulations, Austin branch employees receive 18 days annual PTO due to Texas regional policy (differs from NY HQ 15 days PTO). Paid time off accrues on a pro-rata basis per pay period up to the annual maximum limit of 18 days. To ensure operational continuity across technical engineering and customer support operations, core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 CST. All full-time employees must be actively online and reachable during these core hours. Furthermore, as part of the Austin facility workplace wellness initiative, the company provides an on-site cafeteria subsidy of $15 per business day, credited directly to the employee's physical access badge for use at campus dining locations. Unused daily cafeteria credits do not roll over or convert to cash.

### Section 3: Approval Hierarchy & Operational Workflow
PTO requests and working hour adjustments must be recorded in the HR Information System (HRIS) adhering to the following approval matrix:
1. **Short-Term PTO (1 to 3 consecutive days)**: Requires Direct Team Manager approval at least 48 hours in advance.
2. **Extended PTO (4 to 10 consecutive days)**: Requires Direct Team Manager sign-off followed by Austin Site Director approval at least 2 weeks in advance.
3. **Major Leave (GreaterThan 10 consecutive days)**: Requires Austin Site Director and VP of Human Resources authorization at least 30 days prior to the planned leave period.

Overtime hours for non-exempt employees must be pre-approved by the Department Manager before hours are worked.

### Section 4: Exceptions, Compliance Violations & Human Escalations
Failure to maintain presence during mandatory core working hours without approved leave or medical documentation constitutes unexcused absenteeism. Badge sharing or transferring daily cafeteria credits to unauthorized external individuals is considered theft of company property and will result in immediate loss of cafeteria privileges and formal disciplinary action. Any conflicts between Texas state labor guidelines, local municipal mandates, and global enterprise policies cannot be resolved by local site leads and must be escalated directly to the US Regional HR Compliance Lead and Legal Counsel.
